About Comtrack

Twenty years beside families

Comtrack has partnered with Sena Financial Services, RMA Life and Gen-Life to offer funeral and grocery cover from a low monthly fee*. The grocery plan includes the main member, spouse and six children under the age of 21.

Compassion

Comtrack lifts the weight of funeral arrangements. As a member, you receive a dignified funeral service and groceries delivered to your door.

Dignity

Every family receives the same respect, care and attention to detail. The value of the funeral and grocery cover depends on the premium and the age of the deceased.

Transparency

For members over 14, groceries cater for 250 people. For members under 14, groceries cater for 100 people or fewer, depending on the plan type**.

* Terms and conditions apply. ** Benefit levels depend on the plan chosen.

Frequently asked questions

Who is covered?

The grocery plan includes the main member, spouse and up to six children under the age of 21.

Who stands behind the cover?

Comtrack has partnered with Sena Financial Services, RMA Life and Gen-Life.

What do I do when someone passes away?

Call our 24-hour line. We answer every day of the year and will guide you through each step.

How are groceries provided?

Groceries are delivered to your door. Benefits for members over 14 cater for 250 people. Benefits for members under 14 cater for 100 people or fewer, depending on the plan.

How does ordering work?

Add plans and extras to your order, then place it. We email you a receipt and call you to confirm the details and payment.

Can I change my plan later?

Yes. Contact us by phone, email or WhatsApp and we will help you move to a plan that suits your family.

Contact us

Write your message and send it by email or WhatsApp. For urgent help, call our 24-hour line.
